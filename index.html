§ 01Förderung

How the voucher works

The Bildungsgutschein is a decision your caseworker makes, not a form you download. Three appointments stand between you and a funded seat — we prepare you for all of them.

  1. 01

    Book the caseworker appointment

    Tell your Arbeitsvermittler you want a Weiterbildung in IT security and ask for a Beratungstermin zur Qualifizierung. Registered as unemployed, at risk of losing your job, or in Kurzarbeit — all three qualify you to ask.

    Bring: CV · certificates · our programme sheet

  2. 02

    Make the case for the measure

    Funding is granted when retraining plausibly ends your unemployment. We send you a one-page justification with the measure number, the hour count and the labour-market reasoning your caseworker needs on file.

    We supply: Maßnahmenummer · Kostenaufstellung

  3. 03

    Hand us the voucher

    The voucher names a validity window and a region — usually three months. Send us a photo of it, we invoice the agency directly, and your seat is confirmed for the next cohort inside that window.

    Deadline: voucher must be redeemed before expiry

No voucher? There are three other routes.

The Deutsche Rentenversicherung funds retraining after illness or injury. The Berufsförderungsdienst covers departing Bundeswehr personnel. And employers can co-fund places for staff in Kurzarbeit under the Qualifizierungschancengesetz. We handle the paperwork for all three.

§ 07Aufnahme

From first enquiry to first class

Roughly four weeks, most of which is agency processing time rather than ours.

  1. Day 0

    Eligibility check

    A 15-minute call. We establish which funding route applies to you and send the programme sheet your caseworker needs.

  2. Day 2–5

    Aptitude test and interview

    A logic and pattern assessment — no IT knowledge tested — then a 30-minute conversation about what you want to do next.

  3. Day 5–25

    Voucher issued

    Your caseworker decides. This is the slow step; we stay reachable for any documentation the agency asks for.

  4. Day 25–30

    Onboarding

    Laptop shipped if needed, lab accounts issued, pre-course Linux primer, and you meet your cohort before day one.
