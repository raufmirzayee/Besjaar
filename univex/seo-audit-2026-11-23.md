# Univex Shop (univexbv.com): SEO and content check, 23 Nov 2026

**Scope:**
- The live site: 546 sitemap URLs. 229 pages were crawled in all five languages; 38 more got rate-limited (HTTP 429), which is not a site error.
- The admin data: 32 products, the collections, pages and 58 blog articles.

**Not covered:** rankings, clicks, indexing status and backlinks. Those need Google Search Console, which isn't connected here.

## Verdict

**The technical side and the on-page basics are in very good shape**, better than most small Shopify shops. There's no technical problem holding the site back.

What holds it back is **trust and authority**:
- no reviews;
- very few online orders so far;
- a broad mix of unrelated products;
- 58 blog articles published in one week.

A few cheap on-page fixes remain, the homepage title above all.

## What to improve, most important first

### High

1. **The homepage title has no keywords.** It is `Univex BV – Univex Shop` in all five languages, and the H1 is `Univex Shop`. This is the strongest page on the site and it says nothing about what you sell.
   - Proposed nl: `Univex Shop | Douchekoppen, zaklampen & handige gadgets`
   - de: `Univex Shop | Duschköpfe, Taschenlampen & praktische Gadgets`
   - en: `Univex Shop | Shower heads, flashlights & handy gadgets`
   - fr: `Univex Shop | Pommeaux de douche, lampes torches & gadgets`
   - es: `Univex Shop | Alcachofas de ducha, linternas y gadgets`
2. **No reviews (0 in Judge.me).** Reviews give the star rating in Google results and are the biggest trust signal for a small shop. Do these three:
   - Turn on Judge.me's automatic review-request email.
   - Import any marketplace reviews you have (e.g. bol.com).
   - Ask the last customers personally.
3. **13 URLs have no meta description**, so Google invents one:
   - contact and FAQ pages: `/pages/contact`, `/pages/veelgestelde-vragen`, `/de/pages/kontakt`, `/de/pages/haeufige-fragen`, `/fr/pages/contactez-nous`, `/en/pages/faq`;
   - blog index pages: `/blogs/news`, `/blogs/nieuws`, `/de/blogs/tipps-ratgeber`, `/de/blogs/neuigkeiten`;
   - `/collections/frontpage`, `/de/collections/startseite`;
   - Shopify's own `/agents.md`, which can be ignored.
4. **The new "Producten" page (`/collections/all`) has no SEO title or description.**
5. **The "Aanbiedingen" collection text is wrong.** It says the crossed-out price is the lowest price of the last 30 days, which isn't true for the Black Friday prices. The collection is unpublished today; fix the text before it's used again.

### Medium

6. **The brand appears twice in 17 product titles**, e.g. `Besjaar Douchekop met Filter en Slang (10 standen) | Univex – Univex Shop`. The theme already adds "– Univex Shop", so drop "| Univex" from the SEO titles.
   - 95 of 229 titles are longer than about 65 characters. Most only lose the "– Univex Shop" ending in Google, which is harmless.
7. **The Black Friday article contradicts the prices.** `black-friday-2026-veilig-online-shoppen` explains the 30-day lowest-price rule to readers, while the crossed-out Black Friday prices are 10% above the regular price. Readers can check this. Either soften that part of the article or take it offline until 1 Dec.
8. **Products with too few photos:**
   - Airstyler: 1 photo;
   - Glazen Waterkoker Retro: 1 photo;
   - Waterkoker met temperatuurinstelling: 2 photos.
   Aim for 5 or more: product, in use, details, size, box contents.
9. **Blog quality over quantity.**
   - All 58 articles were published between 7 and 12 Oct. Google is wary of large batches of similar articles. Add your own photos and practical experience to the best 10–15 (douche, zaklamp, waterkoker), and link them to the products.
   - Dated seasonal posts (wintertijd 2026, herfstvakantie 2026, halloween 2026) go stale after the date. Update them each year or redirect them.
   - Two news posts have no image.
   - Several SEO titles use English-style Title Case ("Hoeveel Lumen Heeft een Zaklamp Nodig?"). Dutch uses sentence case: "Hoeveel lumen heeft een zaklamp nodig?"
10. **The frontpage collection is in the sitemap** (`/collections/frontpage`, `/de/collections/startseite`). It's a thin page (about 100 words) with no description. Hide it from search engines.

### Low

11. **Empty alt text on theme card images.** About 2,700 of 5,600 images seen across the crawl have `alt=""`: the same product cards repeated on many pages, plus the decorative logo. The product name sits next to each card, so the effect is small. A theme fix (use the product title as alt) helps Google Images a little.
12. **Two domains, one brand.** besjaar.eu and univexbv.com both sell the Besjaar shower heads, so they compete for "Besjaar douchekop" searches. The text overlap is low, so this isn't a duplicate-content penalty. Decide which site should rank for Besjaar, and link from the other to it.
