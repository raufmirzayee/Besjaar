# Collection descriptions — rewrite (13 Oct 2026)

Removes the claims that the filter takes limescale (*kalk*) out of the water and makes water softer. The product pages say the opposite. The text below uses the product pages' own wording for what the filter does: it holds back particles and breaks down free chlorine.
