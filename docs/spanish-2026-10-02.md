Preview theme: `Besjaar – Español 2 okt (preview)` (id 201790030151), duplicated from
the live theme 201672458567. View it at `https://www.besjaar.eu/es?preview_theme_id=201790030151`.

## Theme (preview theme; goes live when the theme is published)

- `locales/es.json`: all 1,829 theme strings in Spanish. Every `{{ }}` / `%…%`
  placeholder and HTML tag was checked against `en.default.json`.
- The language selector listed only nl/en/de/fr. Spanish was filtered out, so it
  never appeared in the selector even though the language is published. Fixed in
  `snippets/besjaar-ui-localization.liquid`.
- 38 sections, snippets and scripts had hardcoded per-language text. Spanish fell
  through to the Dutch `else` branch. Each now has a `when 'es'` branch:
  - cart drawer, cart page
  - header mega menu, footer
  - product page, product FAQ
  - B2B page, guides, welcome offer
  - SEO titles and descriptions, `og:locale es_ES`
  - JS title maps in `besjaar-ui-core.js`
- All 39 uploads checksum-verified on the preview theme.

## Not translated on purpose — needs the owner/legal first

- **Refund policy (Shopify policy + page):** the text is garbled. A sentence is
  cut ("…een and" … "ere noodzakelijke oplossing") with another block pasted in
  the middle. The two parts contradict each other: "you pay return costs" vs
  "we send a return label", and sale items are excluded while the EU 14-day
  right is also mentioned.
- **Shipping policy:** says free shipping above €25 and €4.99 below. The site
  says free from €20.99.
- **Terms of service:** "We give no warranties" conflicts with the statutory
  consumer warranty (wettelijke garantie). Shipping is described as
  "within the Netherlands" only.
- **Contact information vs legal notice:** two different entities
  (SelectStore B.V. with a gmail address vs Univex B.V.).
- **Privacy policy:** fine to translate once the entity question is settled.
- Once the Dutch texts are fixed and approved, translate them (ideally reviewed by a
  Spanish-speaking professional).
- **Page `besjaar` (handle `besjaar`):** an unrelated "NORDLICHT" demo page with
  invented reviews ("Mark V. – Geverifieerd", "4,6/5 · 1.200+ reviews"). It was not
  translated. Delete or unpublish it.
- **Filter lifespan:** the Dutch collection text and guide articles say "a cartridge
  lasts up to ten months". Product data says 3–5 months per filter and up to 10
  months with the 2-pack. The Spanish text uses the product-data wording. The
  Dutch/English source should be aligned.

Product metafield copy (highlights, specs, box contents) is shown only on the Dutch
storefront by design. Spanish, like en/de/fr, gets the generated equivalents.
